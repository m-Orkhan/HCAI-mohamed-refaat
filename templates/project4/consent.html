Information for Participants

What is this study?

You will be asked to express which movies you would rather watch, using two different interfaces. The purpose is to compare how well each interface captures a person's film preferences. There are no right or wrong answers.

How long does it take?

About 20 minutes: two blocks of five minutes each, two short questionnaires, and a final set of 20 comparisons.

What data is collected?

Your choices between movies, how long each choice took, and your questionnaire answers. You are identified only by a randomly generated code. We do not collect your name, email address, or IP address.

Your rights

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time and delete everything you have contributed using the "Withdraw and delete my data" button, which is available on every screen. You may request access to your data or its deletion later using your participant code. You may lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

Storage and retention

Data is stored pseudonymously and would be deleted 30 days after the conclusion of the study. The legal basis for processing is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time without giving reasons.
